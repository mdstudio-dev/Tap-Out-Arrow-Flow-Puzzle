Privacy Policy – Tap Out: Arrow Flow Puzzle
Last updated: 24 October 2026
Welcome to Tap Out: Arrow Flow Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you play Tap Out: Arrow Flow Puzzle.
1. Information We Collect and Store Locally
Tap Out: Arrow Flow Puzzle stores your gameplay progress (including your current level, star ratings, earned hints, statistics, color themes, unlocked trophies, and settings such as sound volume and hardcore mode) locally on your device using AndroidX DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. AdMob may automatically collect and use certain information to serve ads, including:
•
Advertising ID (GAID)
•
IP address and approximate location data
•
Device and network information
•
App interaction and ad performance data
For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.
3. Consent Management (GDPR / EEA & UK)
If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or other regions subject to privacy regulations, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to obtain your consent for personalized or non-personalized advertising before displaying ads. You can update or withdraw your privacy and consent choices at any time through the Settings menu inside the app.
4. Children's Privacy
Tap Out: Arrow Flow Puzzle is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
5. Data Deletion and Your Rights
You can reset your device's advertising ID at any time through your Android system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all locally saved game data and progress.
6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
